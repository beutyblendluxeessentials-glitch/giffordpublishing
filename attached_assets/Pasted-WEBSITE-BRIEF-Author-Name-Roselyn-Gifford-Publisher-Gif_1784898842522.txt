About Roselyn Gifford
Roselyn Gifford is an author who writes books that explore healing, resilience, self-worth, and personal growth. Through heartfelt nonfiction and emotionally rich storytelling, she creates space for readers to pause, reflect, and rediscover hope in life's most difficult seasons.
Her writing is grounded in honesty rather than perfection, offering encouragement to those navigating heartbreak, grief, anxiety, change, or the quiet work of becoming whole again.
Roselyn believes that healing is rarely a straight path. Her books remind readers that even in moments of uncertainty, there is strength in continuing, grace in beginning again, and courage in choosing hope one day at a time.
When she isn't writing, she is developing new resources designed to inspire growth, reflection, and meaningful change.
Author Mission
To write books that help people feel seen, understood, and encouraged through life's hardest seasons.
Vision
To become a trusted voice in emotional healing and personal growth, creating books that continue to comfort, inspire, and transform readers around the world.

STILL STANDING
Finding Love, Worth and Healing in Your Hardest Seasons
When life leaves you broken, it's easy to believe you'll never feel whole again. Still Standing is a compassionate guide for anyone navigating heartbreak, disappointment, loss, or emotional pain. Through heartfelt reflections, practical encouragement, and gentle wisdom, Roselyn Gifford reminds readers that healing is not about becoming who you were before, but discovering the strength to move forward with hope, courage, and self-worth. This book is an invitation to rise, one step at a time, even when the journey feels uncertain.
STILL HEALING
30 Days of Deep Healing, One Lesson at a Time
Healing doesn't happen overnight, and it doesn't have to. Still Healing is a 30-day guided journey designed to help readers slow down, reflect, and reconnect with themselves after life's most difficult seasons. Each day offers thoughtful insights, practical exercises, affirmations, and gentle encouragement to support emotional healing and personal growth. Whether you're recovering from heartbreak, grief, disappointment, or simply learning to embrace yourself again, this book provides a steady companion for the journey ahead.
STILL WORTHY
Reclaiming Your Worth, One Truth at a Time
Your worth has never depended on what you've been through, what you've lost, or what others have said about you. Still Worthy invites readers to let go of self-doubt, silence the inner critic, and rediscover the value that has always existed within them. Through honest reflections and empowering truths, Roselyn Gifford encourages readers to rebuild confidence, embrace their identity, and live from a place of self-respect, courage, and authenticity.
STILL BREATHING
Finding Peace, Calm and Presence After Life Has Knocked the Breath Out of You
Some seasons leave us feeling overwhelmed, exhausted, and unsure how to keep going. Still Breathing is a gentle reminder that even in the middle of life's storms, peace is still possible. Through reflections on anxiety, emotional overwhelm, grief, and everyday struggles, this book encourages readers to slow down, breathe deeply, and find moments of calm amid the chaos. It is a compassionate companion for anyone seeking hope, presence, and the courage to take the next breath.
