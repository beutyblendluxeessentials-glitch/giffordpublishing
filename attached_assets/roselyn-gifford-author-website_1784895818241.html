About the Author

A quiet kind of storytelling

Roselyn Gifford grew up in a house with more books than furniture, and she has spent every year since trying to add to that particular kind of clutter. Her novels return again and again to the same terrain: small towns, overgrown gardens, and the women who tend both back to life.

In 2021 she founded Gifford Publishing to bring her stories directly to readers, without losing the care that goes into every page. She lives outside a town much like the ones she writes about, with two dogs, a leaning greenhouse, and a manuscript that is always slightly behind schedule.

The Orchard at Wren’s End

When Margaret Wren inherits her grandmother’s failing orchard, she plans to sell it within the month. Instead, she finds herself pruning trees she doesn’t understand, mending fences with a town she left behind, and slowly remembering why she used to love the smell of cut grass in October.
