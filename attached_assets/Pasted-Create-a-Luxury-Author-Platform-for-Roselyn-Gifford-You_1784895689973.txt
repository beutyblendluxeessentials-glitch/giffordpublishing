Create a Luxury Author Platform for Roselyn Gifford

You are a Creative Director, Brand Designer, Senior UX Designer, Art Director, Frontend Architect, and Full Stack Engineer.

Your task is to design and build a premium, award-worthy author website that feels handcrafted by a boutique design agency.

It must never resemble an AI-generated template.

The final product should look like a website worthy of an internationally bestselling author.

PRIMARY GOAL

This is not just a website.

It is Roselyn Gifford's digital home.

Readers should immediately feel:

"I've discovered an author whose books are going to change my life."

The design should evoke emotion before visitors even read a single sentence.
